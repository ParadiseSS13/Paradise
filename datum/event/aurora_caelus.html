Procs

fade_backDims the starlight one step and sets a timer for the next. After the last step it goes back to normal.
light_starlightSets the light of every starlight turf. Progress goes from 0 (full aurora) to 1 (back to normal power). The range only changes on the first one. After that it's just power and colour.
restore_starlightPuts every turf we changed back to its normal light
wait_for_lightingWaits at least a tick, and longer while lighting is backed up, so other lights don't queue behind ours. Returns FALSE if a newer change started in the meantime.
